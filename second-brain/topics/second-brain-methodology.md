# Topic: Second Brain Methodology

What I believe about building and running a personal knowledge system.

## Current view

Capture cheaply, process weekly, organize retrieval by topic rather than
by source type. The point of the system is accumulating my own take on
things, not archiving sources. See the founding note for the full
reasoning.
